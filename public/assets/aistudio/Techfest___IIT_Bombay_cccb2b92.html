About Robowars

The arena is set, the machines are armed, and the battle for glory is about to begin! Techfest, IIT Bombay presents International Robowars, where the world’s most formidable fighting robots enter the arena for an all-out battle of power, precision, and strategy. Expect earth-shaking impacts, jaw-dropping takedowns and edge-of-your-seat action as metal meets metal in a relentless quest for the ultimate International Robowars Champion Title. With electrifying artist performances adding to the frenzy, get ready for an explosive spectacle where every second counts, every clash roars and only one machine reigns supreme!
